FP-001  FIRST-PRINCIPLES DERIVATION
Written 2026-09-20. Derived from the objective alone. No findings from RCA-001 used.
Every step is marked DERIVED (follows from the step above), MEASURED (a number from this
repo, command reproducible), or ASSUMED (stated so it can be challenged).

================================================================================
STEP 1 - STATE THE FUNCTION
================================================================================
DERIVED. The objective is "land the right media candidate on the right beat." As a
function:

    f(beat) -> a visual treatment that asserts what the beat asserts

A beat is a span of narration that makes an assertion. A treatment is a parameterised
visual that, once filled with data, also makes an assertion. So this is not search,
ranking, or recommendation. It is matching one assertion against another.

================================================================================
STEP 2 - WHAT MUST BE TRUE FOR AN ANSWER TO EXIST
================================================================================
DERIVED. A treatment can assert what the beat asserts only if it can physically carry
that assertion. Decomposing what an assertion commits you to:

    ARITY        how many things it is about
    MEASURE      what kind of quantity or relation it carries
                 (magnitude, share of a whole, rank, change over time, none)
    READABILITY  what a viewer must be able to see to verify it
    PROHIBITION  what it must not imply

If a treatment fails any of these, no amount of preference ordering rescues it. It is
not a worse answer; it is not an answer.

CONSEQUENCE: matching is a FILTER, not a RANK. Treating it as a ranking problem invents
a continuum where there is a binary, which is how a list gets sorted by something
irrelevant when the relevant signal runs out.

================================================================================
STEP 3 - THE FEASIBLE SET, AND WHY ITS SIZE IS THE FIRST QUESTION
================================================================================
DERIVED. For a beat b, let S(b) be the set of treatments that can carry its assertion.
|S(b)| >= 0, and the correct behaviour of the system is different in each regime:

    |S| = 0        No selection is possible. The only honest outputs are: source or
                   build something new, or change the beat. Returning a list here is
                   a lie dressed as an answer.
    |S| = 1 to ~4  Selection is trivial. Show all of them. Any machinery for variety,
                   de-duplication or spread is solving a problem that does not exist.
    |S| large      Selection matters. Variety and de-duplication earn their place.

ASSUMED: the library is finite and fixed at selection time, while narration is
unbounded. This is what makes |S| = 0 a real and recurring state rather than an edge
case.

So the first question the system must answer for every beat is not "which is best."
It is "how many can do this at all, and do I know?"

================================================================================
STEP 4 - MEASURE |S| ON THE REAL CORPUS
================================================================================
MEASURED. 40 beats, 421 template records, 642 job bindings.
Applying only hard constraints - the beat's stated arity against the template's
declared capacity, at the standing +/-33% tolerance:

    median feasible set, counting only templates that DECLARE a capacity ....  7
    beats with 0 feasible ..................................................  6 of 40
    beats with <= 3 feasible ............................................... 11 of 40
    beats with > 12 feasible ............................................... 15 of 40

The distribution is bimodal. Two regimes, not one. And they split cleanly by what kind
of beat it is:

    beat states a perceptibility requirement, and is numeric   23 beats   median  4
    beat states a requirement, not numeric                      7 beats   median 13
    beat states no requirement, numeric                         6 beats   median 19
    beat states no requirement, not numeric                     4 beats   median 17

DERIVED FROM THIS: the library is abundant precisely where the beat does not care, and
scarce precisely where it does. The 23 demanding, numeric beats are the majority, and
they are the substance of the documentary - they carry the claims. They have a median
of four viable treatments and six of them have none.

By narration time: scarce beats (<=3 feasible) cover 170s; rich beats (>12) cover 148s.
Nearly half the placed narration sits in the regime where selection barely exists.

================================================================================
STEP 5 - A FOURTH REGIME WE DID NOT ANTICIPATE
================================================================================
MEASURED. |S| is not always computable. Across the 23 demanding numeric beats:

    total bound candidates ................................. 583
    pass arity, counting undeclared capacity as a pass ..... 436
    pass arity on a DECLARED capacity ...................... 124
    survive only because capacity is undeclared ............ 312   = 71%

    records in the pool declaring no capacity at all ....... 221 of 421 = 52%

DERIVED: there is a fourth state alongside 0 / few / many, and it is the largest one.

    |S| UNKNOWN    the template records do not say what they can do, so the system
                   cannot rule them in or out.

The standing rule "unknowns never disqualify" is correct as a rule - a missing field is
not evidence of unsuitability. But its consequence at 52% coverage is that most of the
surviving set is not known to survive. What looks like abundance is mostly ignorance.

================================================================================
STEP 6 - THE CEILING
================================================================================
DERIVED. No matcher can outperform the information it is given. With 52% of the library
silent about its own capacity, any selector faces a forced choice:

    admit unknowns    -> returns treatments that cannot carry the beat
    exclude unknowns  -> discards over half the library unseen

Both are wrong, and no algorithm resolves it, because the missing thing is data, not
logic. This bounds the achievable precision of every design we could write, before we
write any of them.

CONSEQUENCE: effort spent on the matcher is capped by this ceiling. Effort spent
raising the ceiling is not.

================================================================================
STEP 7 - WHAT WOULD RAISE THE CEILING
================================================================================
DERIVED. From step 2, a beat can only require four kinds of thing: arity, measure,
readability, prohibition. That is a small closed set, and it is the same set on both
sides. A beat states requirements in it. A treatment declares capabilities in it.

If both sides speak it, matching becomes a set operation:

    S(b) = { t : t.capabilities  satisfies  b.requirements }

    arity        t.capacity within tolerance of b.entity_count
    measure      b.measure in t.measures
    readability  b.must_be_readable subset of t.can_show
    prohibition  b.prohibitions disjoint from t.implies

Deterministic. No pairwise judgment. No ranking. No per-beat model call. Fully
explainable: for any rejected treatment, the failing clause is nameable.

================================================================================
STEP 8 - WHERE A MODEL IS ACTUALLY REQUIRED
================================================================================
DERIVED. Filling that vocabulary is judgment over prose, which no rule can do. But it
is judgment about ONE artifact at a time, not about a pair:

    per template  read its description and declare its capabilities     421 calls-worth
    per beat      read the narration and state its requirements          n calls-worth

That is O(n + m), once each. Pairwise judgment is O(n x m) and must be re-run whenever
either side changes.

DERIVED, AND THIS IS THE LOAD-BEARING PART: a capability declaration is a property of
the template alone. It does not depend on the job taxonomy, the niche, or the script.
It is computed once and is correct forever unless the template changes. A pairwise
binding is a property of the pair, so it must be recomputed for every new job, every
new niche, and every change to either vocabulary.

Capabilities port across niches. Pairings do not.

================================================================================
STEP 9 - WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES WHEN |S| = 0
================================================================================
DERIVED. From step 3, |S| = 0 is a legitimate and recurring output, not a failure. The
correct response is a supply request: this beat needs a treatment that holds N things
and makes a share readable, and none exists.

MEASURED: 6 of 40 beats are in this state today, and they are silently receiving slates
of up to 12 treatments that cannot carry them.

DERIVED: at 20 videos/day across niches, the gap between what narration demands and what
the library holds is the quantity that determines throughput. A system that measures
that gap and emits it as a queue closes the loop. A system that pads it with
near-misses hides the one number that would tell you what to build next.

================================================================================
STEP 10 - THE DESIGN THIS ARRIVES AT
================================================================================
DERIVED from steps 1-9.

    1  CAPABILITY DECLARATION   model, once per template, ported across all niches.
                                Fills the closed vocabulary from step 7.
    2  REQUIREMENT EXTRACTION   model, once per beat. Already largely exists: beats
                                carry entity_count, must_be_perceptible, would_be_a_lie.
                                Needs stating in the same vocabulary as (1).
    3  FEASIBILITY              deterministic set operation. Produces S(b) plus, for
                                every excluded treatment, the clause it failed.
    4  BRANCH ON |S|            0        -> supply request, no slate
                                1 to ~4  -> show all, no diversification
                                large    -> diversify, and only here
    5  COVERAGE REPORT          the aggregate of (4) over a script: what the library
                                cannot say. This is the queue that grows the library.

WHAT THIS REMOVES: per-job binding as the primary index, ranking of any kind, and all
diversification machinery outside the large-|S| branch.

WHAT IT KEEPS: the 20 jobs, as a useful label for the beat and for rotation and style
downstream - but not as the key that selection turns on.

================================================================================
STEP 11 - HOW TO KNOW THIS IS WRONG
================================================================================
Stated in advance, so it can fail rather than be defended.

  T1  Vocabulary closure. Take 30 beats not used in this derivation. If their
      requirements cannot be expressed in the four categories of step 7 without adding
      a fifth, step 7 is wrong.
  T2  Declarability. Take 30 templates. If a capability declaration cannot be filled
      from the written description alone, step 8 is wrong and the ceiling in step 6
      cannot be raised by this route.
  T3  Ceiling. After declaring capability on a sample, the share of arity-survivors
      that are "unknown" must fall well below 71%. If it does not, the data gap is not
      closable and the whole derivation changes.
  T4  Regime split. |S| computed on declared data must remain bimodal. If it turns out
      unimodal and large once capability is declared, step 3's branching is unnecessary
      and the simpler design wins.
  T5  Agreement. Two independent runs of capability declaration on the same template
      must produce the same declaration. If they do not, the vocabulary is too loose to
      be a filter.

================================================================================
STEP 12 - WHAT THIS DERIVATION ASSUMES AND DOES NOT PROVE
================================================================================
  - ASSUMED that the written description is sufficient to declare capability. T2 tests
    it. If descriptions are too thin, the real work is enriching the library records,
    and no design above helps until that is done.
  - NOT PROVEN that the four categories in step 2 are complete. They are derived from
    what an assertion commits you to, which is reasoning, not measurement. T1 tests it.
  - NOT ADDRESSED: style, colour, rotation, and staging. These are downstream of
    feasibility and this derivation says nothing about them.
  - NOT COSTED. Step 8 claims O(n+m) beats O(n x m); it does not put a price on either.
