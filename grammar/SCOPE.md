# Template scope restrictions

Templates whose use is limited to a specific content type, regardless of what their structure
could otherwise serve. User instruction, 2026-09-18.

| template | scenes | scoped to |
|---|---|---|
| `archive3-grunge-lyric-video-template` | 2 | **lyrics only** |
| `archive3-horizontal-music-players-with-lyric-vol-2` | 5 | **lyrics only** |
| `archive3-search-bar-business-timeline-...` | 1 | **timelines only** |

A scope restriction is a hard filter, unlike style or colour. It applies before job matching,
not after. A lyric template is out of scope for a proportion-of-cohort beat no matter how well
its structure fits, because the content class is wrong.

This is the one place where a template's subject matter does limit it, and it is an explicit
user decision rather than an inference from the sample. Do not generalise it: the standing rule
remains that sample content never constrains a template.
