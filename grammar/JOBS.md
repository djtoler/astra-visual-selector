# Communication jobs — derived from the Year Seventeen corpus

Every passage classified by what it asks a visual to do. Derived, not invented: each job
carries the passages that produced it. This is the closed set the grammar binds to.

## What this says

**The original twenty jobs cover thirty Year Seventeen passages.** The first cross-story
acceptance package adds `attributed_quote`, derived from three narrator-read quotations
whose speaker contract requires the quoted person and source to remain perceptible. Ten
of the original twenty appear more than once, and five
appear three or more times. The head is concentrated and the tail is long.

**Roughly two in three beats want a data treatment.** The rest want a face, a line of text,
a piece of footage, or a held question. A selector that only reasons about charts is solving
two thirds of the problem.

**The jobs are domain-independent and the subject matter is not.** `one_vs_aggregate` is the
same job whether the entity is a rapper, a football club, a founder or a country. That is the
whole argument for binding templates to jobs rather than searching per beat: the job set
ports across niches, the catalog does not.

**`explain_the_encoding` is the one nobody builds for.** When a video introduces a chart it
must first teach the chart. It happens once per video, it is structural, and no template in
the current catalog is described as doing it.

## How the grammar uses this

Per niche, bind each job to a small set of natively verified treatments. Selection becomes:
classify the beat into a job, then draw from that job's bound set with rotation. Catalog
search disappears. Native verification happens once per treatment instead of once per pick.

New Envato templates get onboarded by binding them to a job and verifying once. The catalog
can grow without the decision cost growing.

## Honest limits

This is one video in one niche. The job set will grow when a second niche lands, and the
right test is how much: if a business-history script needs five new jobs, the set is roughly
closed and the approach holds. If it needs twenty, it does not.

Passage 4 and 13 each carry two jobs, so beats do not map one-to-one onto passages. The unit
of binding is the beat, not the passage.
