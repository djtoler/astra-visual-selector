THUMBNAILS. Media cards were object-fit:cover in a 1:1 box, so a tall portrait got
cropped top and bottom — on a cutout that is the head and the feet, the two things
that say what the framing is. Media now uses contain. Templates keep cover: they
are 16:9 clips in a 16:9 card, so nothing is lost there.

B-ROLL. A checkbox on every beat. It does not filter the still list — it swaps in a
separately fetched, video-only set. Filtering the stills by media_type would have
shown almost nothing, because a clip that serves a beat is usually not a portrait
of anyone on it. Framing is deliberately not applied to b-roll either: a framing
tag describes a still crop, and asking a clip to be a headshot would empty an
already thin set.

The state is saved per beat, so a ticked b-roll box is a recorded judgment about
what this beat wants, not just a view.

AND THE CARDS PLAY. B-roll cards were going to be stills, which is judging the
wrong thing — footage is motion. There are only 31 distinct b-roll videos across
all 40 beats, so every one is now a real clip with a play button.
