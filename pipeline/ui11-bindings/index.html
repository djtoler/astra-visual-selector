Bound, Rejected, Remaining

Why some slates look irrelevant. A beat draws from the templates bound to its job. Everything you rejected is removed from later slates — correctly — and on a thin job that empties the binding, so what is left is the residue you liked least.

A rejection only counts against the job it was bound to. 74 rejections on these beats were of templates bound to some other job — admitted by a scope rule, a match-cut admission or a user instruction — and they are not charged against this binding. Six beats have three or fewer left.

Stills, not clips: this is an audit of what happened, not a place to judge a treatment. Click any row to open that beat.
