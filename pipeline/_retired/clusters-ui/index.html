Mechanism Clusters

A cluster is a set of treatments the pipeline judged to be the same idea at the same scale. Clusters cap how much of one idea can reach a slate: at most four members of any cluster, and separately at most one scene per template family.

The grouping is derived from the mechanism text, which is sometimes three words long — so it gets things wrong. Press the small play button to watch a treatment; it stops at the end. Select cards and move them to fix the grouping. Your edits are authoritative and override what the pipeline derived.
