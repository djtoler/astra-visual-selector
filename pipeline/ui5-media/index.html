Media Slot Review

Which asset fills this slot. Each brief shows a GROUP tier — one asset carrying every named entity, so one slot serves them all — then each entity on its own.

Click a card to pick it. Press W on a card when it is the wrong person — that is not beat-specific, it records that the asset is not that entity anywhere, and the next candidate takes its place. The search is deliberately loose, so W is what makes it get cleaner.

The show filter up top narrows what is on screen — a way to look, not a way to lose a decision. Anything already picked stays visible under every filter.

Say what is wrong. Every entity has a feedback box and every brief has its own. A picked card tells us what worked; a note is the only thing that can tell us what was never offered.
